Chicken Lollipop

Description

Chicken lollipop is a popular Indo-Chinese appetizer made with frenched chicken drumettes, deep fried till crisp and tossed in a tangy, spicy garlic sauce. A party favorite, best served hot.

Ingredients

  • ½ kg chicken drummettes
  • Oil for frying
  • ¼ to ½ tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • ¾ tbsp minced ginger
  • ¾ tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Spring onions, for garnish

Steps

  1. In a bowl, add the chicken drummettes along with chili powder, garam masala and salt.
  2. Sprinkle cornstarch over the chicken and rub well to coat evenly.
  3. Heat oil in a deep pan until hot.
  4. Fry the chicken on medium heat, turning occasionally, until crisp and golden.
  5. Remove the fried chicken and drain on paper towels.
  6. In a separate pan, heat a little oil and sauté the minced ginger and garlic until aromatic.
  7. Add the chopped onions and fry until soft and transparent.
  8. Stir in the soy sauce, vinegar and sugar, mixing well.
  9. Let the sauce simmer for a minute and thicken slightly.
  10. Add the fried chicken to the pan and toss until well coated in the sauce.
  11. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
